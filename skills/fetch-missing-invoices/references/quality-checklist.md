# Quality checklist — verify before finishing a run

Before closing out a fetch-missing-invoices run, verify every line below. This is the
full pre-completion audit for the flow; `SKILL.md` carries the rules being checked, this
file carries the checklist itself.

- If no `well_*` tool was in the toolset, the user was pointed at `https://api.wellapp.ai/v1/mcp` and the flow stopped there.
- Every step ran in order; each route came from the routing table's key; the only steps skipped were the two the flow skips by rule — the bank step when a selected month already holds bank transactions, and the connect step when the pick names no connector.
- No turn rendered two widget cards; every card ended its turn with one card-pointing line — the missing-invoices card included — and no wait call ran before or in the card's turn; every next message was resolved by rule 3 — a prefill taken at its word with no extra verification call, any other message getting one `well_wait_for_selection` call with `timeout_s: 10`, or, at steps 4 and 6 which have no kind of their own, one ask for the Continue click and an ended turn — and nothing was re-asked in text.
- Every `well_list_workspaces` turn ended on the workspace picker — step 1 on a resolved path included, and each pass boundary of a multi-workspace run — so no later read shared a turn with it.
- No "which workspace?" or "which month?" question was asked in text where a card renders; a session pin or selection was reused only when this conversation established it — a leftover was ignored, unmentioned, its picker rendered anyway (rule 8).
- Step 3 was decided on `bank_transaction_count` alone: skipped only where EVERY selected month carried a count above 0, run wherever any selected month carried 0, and run wherever the field was missing for any selected month. `transaction_count` decided nothing, and no absent field was read as a 0.
- When step 3 ran it stopped for its ack — a connected bank included — and moved on only on the "Continue" prefill, a typed continue, a typed skip, or a wait-read ack. The counts it tested came from `define-period`'s hand-off; this flow never called `well_list_periods` itself to fetch them, and it passed `probe: false` so the brick ran no `transactions` probe.
- A selection holding several months was reported month by month: every month named from `periods_covered`, per-month totals taken from `months`, no single `period_label` quoted and no range label composed, and the rows left one per counterparty per month.
- `well_list_missing_invoices` and `well_preview_invoice_fetch` were called with `workspace_id` only — no periods argument — never in the same turn as each other (the gap-list turn ended on step 5's tick-and-Continue line), and a no-selection error went back to step 2, never to a guessed month.
- The vendor pick was in hand before step 7 previewed anything: the card's "Continue" prefill, or one `well_wait_for_selection({ kind: "counterparties", timeout_s: 10 })` call, or the flow's own write on the text-only path. The pick travels as `company_id` and `matched_connector_service_id`; no vendor name was written into it, and no preview stood in for a pick that never came. A wait-read pick whose `selection.workspace_id` was not the pinned workspace was treated as no pick, and the turn asked for the tick on the card now on screen.
- The pick belonged to the gap-list card on screen (rule 10): a pick taken before a month change or before a second gap-list read was taken again, never carried into the preview.
- Step 4 ran on its own read, not on a request: the condition was `well_list_counterparties`' `uncategorized_count` above 0, its denominator was named as the counterparties whose invoices the selected months are still missing rather than as the months' or the workspace's counterparties, `uncategorized_count` was quoted as ROWS rather than as companies, and the step wrote only rows the user confirmed, through `well_update_company`'s `category_ids` replace-set carrying catalog `category_id` values — never a `relationships` input, which that tool's strict schema refuses. Every proposed category came from the catalog read under the carve-out; no catalog was expected in the counterparty result itself, and no unplaceable row got a guessed match. No answer said categorizing a counterparty would surface more missing invoices or change a row's route, no answer called the gap list thin, and `transaction_count` was never presented as the transactions Well examined. A read with nothing to categorize was stated in half a sentence and the flow continued to step 5 in the same turn. A read that DID have something to categorize held the flow at step 4 until the user's continue arrived — no gap list, connect step or preview was called to get around it, and no wait-read stood in for that continue.
- Step 6 ran whenever the pick named a counterparty carrying a `matched_connector_service_id`: `connect-tools` ran with `from_selection: true`, the turn ended on its card, and the result's `scope` read `picked_vendors` rather than a domain catalog. No turn described those connect rows in prose and went straight to the preview, and no `well_wait_for_selection({ kind: "connect_ack" })` call stood in for its click, because that step takes no wait-read. A pick naming no connector skipped the step entirely.
- An `empty` list was resolved on `dropped_groups`: the flow celebrated only when `unknown` and `unnamed_company` were both 0. With either above 0 it said the period holds categorized spend with no named counterparty, claimed nothing over it, and presented the period as complete nowhere.
- No number was quoted for `dropped_groups.bank_internal` or for `dropped_groups.unknown` — each counts the months that held any — and `unnamed_company` was the only one of the three quoted as a quantity.
- Step 7 previewed only: every agent line said nothing has started, the upload and connect lines appeared even at zero, the deploy line and the nothing-started sentence were both present, and no collection, yield, or ETA was claimed — in the chat or in the Well app. Every vendor the collect link cannot take — a null `provider_id`, or one the tool named in `collect_url_omits` — got its own line saying the link starts nothing for it. Its wording came from the agent count alone — no sentence assumed, or denied, that a card was on screen — and no collect link was built by hand.
- The scope line came from `scoped_to_selected_counterparties`: the picked vendors only when it was true, the whole period when it was absent and no `agent_candidates` narrowed the result.
- On failure the flow stopped at that step, named it, gave the workspace link, and did not re-read the same data itself or skip ahead.
- On a multi-workspace run, the queue came from the session block, each pass re-pinned with `well_switch_workspace`, every call carried that pass's `workspace_id`, and each entity got its own recap with nothing merged.
- Every step ran its own brick — `define-workspace`, `define-period`, `connect-bank`, `categorize-counterparties`, `show-missing-invoices`, `connect-tools` at step 6, `deploy-agents` — and an inline fallback was used ONLY where that brick was absent from the toolset, on one of the five steps that carry one (2, 4, 5, 6, 7). No step reimplemented a brick that was installed.
- `well_switch_workspace` was called only to record a choice no card recorded (rule 9): a matched workspace hint, a month hint or typed month, the text-only vendor pick, or a re-pin to a DIFFERENT workspace between passes. The workspace was never re-pinned to the id it already held, the months a Validate click wrote were never re-sent, and the session was never read back through this tool.
- Steps 2 to 7 pinned nothing: no `well_switch_workspace` call sits between the month card and the gap list, and no call carried `workspace_id` or `workspace_ids` as its only field — either one is a pin write. A `counterparties` or `ack` call carries `workspace_id` beside the answer it records, which targets that one call and leaves the pin and the queue alone.
- Step 4's `periods` values came from the period card's prefill or from rule 3's one `well_wait_for_selection({ kind: "periods" })` answer — never from a second `well_switch_workspace` write made to see them echoed.
- A vendor Well holds a connector for was reported as ONE gap with two routes — on the connect step and under its portal in the preview — never as two gaps, and the preview's counts were quoted in the units `counts` states (portals, transactions, counterparties), never added together.
- No forbidden tool was called — `well_get_entity` and `well_get_schema` included — no yaml / JSON / fenced block was printed, and each list or read tool ran once per step. `well_query_records` ran only under its one carve-out: step 4's proposal mode, after the user asked for proposals, on the `categories` root filtered to `category_type: "company"`, in a turn that rendered no other card. It ran nowhere else, and never to rebuild a list a `well_list_*` tool owns.
